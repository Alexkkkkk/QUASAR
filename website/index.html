Cosmic Features

Owner-governed AI controls

Optional risk controls are bounded and owner-governed. Market signals cannot move reserve funds or trigger buybacks.

Auto Burn & Buyback

The source splits transfer fees across burn and ecosystem buckets. Buybacks require an owner-authenticated trigger; they are not automatic.

💰

LP farming prototype

LP tokens can be auto-staked in the source; rewards are claimed explicitly. There is no auto-compounding or guaranteed return.

Built-in CPMM
